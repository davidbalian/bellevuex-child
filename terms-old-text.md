Prior to your arrival, you will receive a unique access code corresponding to your reservation. This code will be valid for the duration of your stay.
Each guest has their personal code for the apartment, as well as the code for building and floor access sent around 14:00 or even earlier if permitted by availability .
The ground floor door is locked after 18:00. if you arrive later, use the code provided!
In case you need our physical presence, you can find us daily at our office from 9:00 AM to 16:00 on the ground floor of 11 Theseos Street.
